Title: Stage_Lobby - Hierarchy & Wiring Guide
SceneName: Stage_Lobby
Purpose: 병원 로비(문/아이템/점프스케어)

Hierarchy (추천)
- StageRoot (StageRoot)
- BG (SpriteRenderer/Image)
- Doors
  - Door_Office (HotspotMove → Stage_Office)
  - Door_Delivery (HotspotMove → Stage_Delivery, requiredFlag=DeliveryUnlocked)
- Items
  - Cross (HotspotInspect → item_cross)
  - Permit (HotspotInspect → item_permit)
  - Letter (HotspotInspect → item_letter, setFlag=ReadAccidentLetter)
- Jumpscare
  - CounterGhost (Trigger → JumpScarePlayer.Play)

Components & Wiring
- StageRoot: 기본
- Doors/Door_Office(HotspotMove): targetStageId = Stage_Office
- Doors/Door_Delivery(HotspotMove): targetStageId = Stage_Delivery (플래그 가드 필요)
- Items/*(HotspotInspect): itemId 설정, CloseUpController로 연결(추후)
- Jumpscare/CounterGhost: 트리거 충돌 시 JumpScarePlayer 호출(추후)

Notes
- 초기 MVP에서는 Inspect/JumpScare를 비활성화하고 Move만 우선 구현 가능
