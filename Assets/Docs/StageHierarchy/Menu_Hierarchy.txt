Title: Stage_Menu - Hierarchy & Wiring Guide
SceneName: Stage_Menu
Purpose: 메인 메뉴(새 게임/계속/설정/종료)

Hierarchy (추천)
- Canvas (Screen Space - Overlay)
  - Panel_BG (Image)
  - MainMenu (empty, MainMenuUI)
    - Buttons
      - Button_New
      - Button_Continue
      - Button_Settings
      - Button_Quit
  - Panel_Settings (inactive, SettingsPanel)

Components & Wiring
- Canvas: CanvasScaler(Scale With Screen Size)
- Panel_BG: Image(color=#000000, a=0.65)
- MainMenu(MainMenuUI):
  - defaultStartStageId = Stage_Front
  - continueButton = Buttons/Button_Continue
  - settingsPanel = Panel_Settings
- Buttons/Button_New: OnClick → MainMenuUI.OnClickNewGame()
- Buttons/Button_Continue: OnClick → MainMenuUI.OnClickContinue()
- Buttons/Button_Settings: OnClick → MainMenuUI.OnClickSettings()
- Buttons/Button_Quit: OnClick → MainMenuUI.OnClickQuit()
- Panel_Settings(SettingsPanel): 슬라이더/토글 배선(선택), 기본 비활성

Notes
- Build Settings에 Stage_Menu 추가(엔트리로 사용 시 Core→Menu 흐름 고려)
- Save/Audio 미구현 시 Continue/Settings는 비활성/간소화 가능

--- 세부 스펙 템플릿 (추후 채움) ---
[Objects & Components]
- Canvas: [Canvas, CanvasScaler, GraphicRaycaster]
- MainMenu(MainMenuUI): [MainMenuUI]
