Title: Stage_Office - Hierarchy & Wiring Guide

Purpose: 관리실(CCTV 점프스케어, 열쇠 획득)

Hierarchy (추천)
- StageRoot (StageRoot)
- BG (SpriteRenderer/Image)
- Props
  - CCTV (HotspotInspect → setFlag=Jumpscare_Office)
  - Docs (HotspotInspect → item_incident_docs)
  - Blueprint (HotspotInspect → item_blueprint)
  - KeyRing (HotspotInspect → item_keyring, setFlag=DeliveryUnlocked)
- BackDoor (HotspotMove → Stage_Lobby)

Components & Wiring

- Props/CCTV: Inspect 시 JumpScarePlayer 호출(추후)
- Props/KeyRing: setFlag=DeliveryUnlocked → Lobby의 Door_Delivery 가드 해제
- BackDoor(HotspotMove): targetStageId = Stage_Lobby

Notes
- 점프스케어는 입력 잠금 윈도우/모노 다운과 함께 구현(추후 단계)
